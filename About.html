About

His Royal Highness (HRH) MBATU II is a respected traditional ruler of the Njah-Etu Fondom in the Meta Clan of Momo Division, in the Northwest Region of Cameroon. As custodian of the customs, traditions, and cultural heritage of the Njah-Etu people, he is committed to promoting peace, unity, good governance, and sustainable community development.

HRH MBATU II advocates for youth empowerment, education, social cohesion, and the preservation of the rich cultural identity of the Meta people. Through his leadership, he continues to encourage dialogue, reconciliation, and collective action aimed at improving the welfare and progress of the Njah-Etu community, while strengthening cooperation with other communities within the Meta Clan.
